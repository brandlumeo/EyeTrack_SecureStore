Refund Policy

Refund and cancellation rules

Refund and cancellation requests are reviewed against order status, product condition, and support verification.

Eligibility

  • Cancellation is generally allowed before dispatch or installation scheduling.
  • Damaged or incorrect item cases should be reported with evidence within 48 hours of delivery.
  • Refund approvals are processed to the original payment method after verification.

Non-Refundable Cases

  • Custom enterprise deployments after implementation start.
  • Misuse, physical tampering, or unauthorized hardware modification.
