Zone 1 · Video door phone

Gate & visitors

See and speak to visitors before you open the gate, from an indoor monitor or, on supported models, your phone.

Check before you buy

  • Where the outdoor unit will mount: gate pillar or front door
  • Cable distance from the gate to the indoor monitor
  • Whether you want to answer calls on your phone
  • Rain and sun cover for the outdoor unit

Around the house

Cover the gate, driveway, parking and side or back entrances, then watch live or play back recordings from anywhere.

Zone 3 · Smart lock

Main door

Open the door with a fingerprint, PIN or card, depending on the model, and stop worrying about spare keys.

Check before you buy

  • Door material: wood, steel or UPVC
  • Door thickness
  • Lock fitted today and which way the handle turns
  • Who needs access: family, staff or guests
