Privacy Policy

How we handle your data

Eyetrack Solutions collects only necessary information to process orders, provide installation support, and communicate service updates.

Data We Collect

We believe in transparency. The following information is collected to ensure the best possible experience and security.

  • Account details such as username and email.
  • Order data including billing, phone number, and delivery address.
  • Technical logs required for security, fraud prevention, and reliability.

How We Use Data

Your data is exclusively used for operational requirements and never sold to third parties.

  • Order fulfillment and customer support.
  • Security monitoring and abuse prevention.
  • Service communication for order and installation lifecycle updates.

Data Protection

Access to operational data is restricted and handled under strict role-based internal controls to prevent unauthorized access. If you have concerns, contact our privacy team directly.
