Shipping Policy

Dispatch & Delivery Standards

Eyetrack Solutions dispatches available products promptly with secure commercial-grade packaging for sensitive surveillance equipment.

Processing & Dispatch

Every piece of security hardware undergoes rigorous packaging and handling checks before leaving our warehouse.

  • Orders are processed dynamically after strict payment confirmation.
  • Standard dispatch typically occurs within 1-2 business days for actively stocked items.
  • Large enterprise or multi-site orders naturally require scheduled freight loading.

Delivery Scope

We work tightly alongside specialized carrier networks to ensure zero transit damage.

  • Exact delivery timelines vary purely by regional coverage and carrier bandwidth.
  • Physical installation routing acts independently and is queued post-delivery.
  • Customers should actively confirm package integrity during acceptance signatures.

Support Tracking

Once dispatched, you will receive tracking pings instantly. For missing freight or anomalous delivery updates, contact operations immediately.

Reach dispatch operations at info@etsgrp.in.
